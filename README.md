# The Lantern Keeper

An Islamic-themed educational puzzle game for children, available on the iOS App Store.

---

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2, 2026

The Lantern Keeper is designed with your child's privacy as a top priority.

### Data We Collect

**None.**

The Lantern Keeper does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information whatsoever. Specifically:

- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
- We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind
- We do not use cookies or any persistent storage that leaves your device
- We do not share data with third parties because there is no data to share
- We do not display advertisements
- We do not include in-app purchases

The app runs entirely on your device and works completely offline.

### Children's Privacy

The Lantern Keeper is designed for children ages 8 and up. Because the app collects no information of any kind, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's guidelines for kids' apps.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since the app collects no data, changes are unlikely.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. Everything runs on your device — no internet connection is needed at any time.

**Are the puzzles randomized?**
Yes. Each playthrough randomly selects a subset of puzzles from a larger pool, so no two playthroughs are exactly the same.

**Is the app suitable for very young children?**
The app is designed for ages 8 and up. Younger children may need help reading some of the longer lessons.

**Will there be more rooms or puzzles?**
We hope to add more content in future updates based on user feedback.
